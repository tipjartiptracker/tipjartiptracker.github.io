About Tip Jar

Tip Jar is a free tip tracker, built by servers, for servers. It has no accounts and no ads, and it never sends your data anywhere.

Install Tip Jar on your phone

Android: Get it on Google Play, or open Tip Jar in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen (or Install app).

iPhone: Open Tip Jar in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen and works offline.

Each phone keeps its own data. To move to a new phone, save a backup here and restore it there.

Your checks

How checks are counted

With paychecks turned on, each check's net pay is split across the shifts in its pay period, based on hours worked. That adds your wages to take-home and your real hourly rate in Graphs and Stats. Tips % of sales stays tips only. A check with no shifts logged in its pay period counts on its pay date.
