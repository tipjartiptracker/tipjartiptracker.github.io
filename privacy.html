Tip Jar

Privacy policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Short version: Tip Jar does not collect, store, share, or sell any of your information. Everything you enter stays on your own phone.

What Tip Jar stores

The shifts, paychecks, notes, goals and settings you enter are saved only in your phone's storage for the Tip Jar app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Tip Jar has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no analytics or tracking.

Backups

When you choose "Save backup," Tip Jar creates a file on your phone. Where that file goes next, such as your downloads, Google Drive, iCloud or an email, is up to you. Tip Jar never uploads it anywhere.

Services Tip Jar loads

To display its fonts and charts, Tip Jar downloads them from Google Fonts and jsDelivr, usually just the first time it opens. Like any website, these services receive basic technical information needed to send the files, such as your IP address and device type. Tip Jar does not send them any of your shift or pay information.

Reminders

If you set up a reminder to log your tips, Tip Jar hands the event to your own calendar app. Your calendar's privacy policy applies to that event.

Tipping the creator

The "Tip the Jar" page links to Venmo, Cash App or PayPal. Tipping is optional and gives you nothing extra in the app. If you send a tip, that payment happens entirely in those services, under their privacy policies. Tip Jar never sees your payment details.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time in Settings under "Delete all data." Uninstalling Tip Jar or clearing its data also erases everything on your phone.

Children

Tip Jar is meant for working adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us through the Google Play listing for Tip Jar.
